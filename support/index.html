Support

Having trouble or have a suggestion? Open an issue and describe what you expected and what happened. Please don’t include personal or health information in a public issue.

Common questions

Is there a tour of the app?

Yes. Manna shows a short tour after setup. You can skip it, and replay it anytime in Settings › About Manna › App tour. Where Manna’s health guidance comes from is listed in Settings › About Manna › Sources.

Do I need an account?

No. Manna uses the Apple ID already on your iPhone. Your logs sync through your own iCloud, so there is nothing to sign up for.

My data isn’t syncing between devices

Make sure you’re signed in to iCloud on each device (Settings › [your name]) and that iCloud Drive is on. If iCloud is off, Manna shows a banner on Home and keeps your data on that device only. Changes usually appear within a few seconds; opening Manna catches up right away.

How do I share with my partner or a caregiver?

Go to Settings › Family sharing, tap Invite partner or caregiver, choose Caregiver (can log) or Viewer (view only), and send the link. They tap it on their iPhone to join. Sharing is part of Manna Family; people you invite don’t pay. Set “Your name” so entries show who logged them.

Is my mom data shared too?

No. Mood, recovery, pregnancy and journal entries are private to you, even when you share a baby.

Is Manna Family a subscription?

No. Manna Family is a one-time purchase ($4.99 in the US). Pay once and keep it forever, with no renewals. Caregivers you invite never pay.

I bought Manna Family on another device

Open Settings › Restore Purchases. Manna Family supports Apple Family Sharing, so someone in your Apple family group can use it too.

How do I use the Live Activity?

Start a nursing, pumping or sleep timer, then lock your phone. The timer appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with Switch side, Pause and Stop buttons. If it doesn’t appear, check Settings › Manna › Live Activities.

How do I choose which baby a widget shows?

Long-press the widget, tap Edit Widget, and pick a baby. Leave it empty to follow the baby selected in the app.

What can I say to Siri?

Try “Start left side nursing in Manna,” “Log a wet diaper in Manna,” “Baby woke up in Manna,” or “When did baby last eat in Manna.” All Manna shortcuts are listed in the Shortcuts app.

The Apple Watch app is empty

Open Manna on your iPhone once and make sure your watch is signed in to the same iCloud account. The watch app is part of Manna Family. If it isn’t installed, open the Watch app on your iPhone and add Manna under Available Apps.

Why don’t I see percentile curves?

How do I get a report for the pediatrician?

Settings › Export & reports. The CSV of all entries is free; the one-page PDF summary is part of Manna Family.

Can Manna use my weight from Apple Health?

Yes. In the Mom tab, open Weight and tap Import from Apple Health to bring in weigh-ins from the last six months (for example, from a smart scale). Turn on Settings › Privacy › Apple Health to also save the weight and water you log in Manna to Health.

Can I add twins?

Yes. When adding a baby, turn on Twins and enter both names. If you’re pregnant, turn on Expecting twins.

How do I change, edit or remove a baby?

Settings › Babies. Tap a baby to edit their details, or swipe left to remove them and all of their logs.

Can I change the buttons on Home?

Yes, for each baby, with Manna Family. Tap the grid menu at the top right of Home › Customize buttons. Add, remove or drag to reorder buttons like Nurse, Bottle, Diaper, Potty or Medicine.

How do I pick a side before a nursing timer starts?

Tap Nurse, choose Left or Right (Manna preselects the side to start on next), then tap Start. Turn on Started earlier if you forgot to start it on time.

How does potty tracking work?

From the grid menu on Home, open Potty training. Choose Pee, Poop or Both, then tap Success, Accident or Tried. Manna shows on-potty and accident counts and success rates for pee and poop separately. Potty training is part of Manna Family.

Can I use Light or Dark mode?

Settings › Appearance › Light & dark. Choose System, Light or Dark.

How do I turn off the Bible verses?

Settings › Scripture › Show KJV verses.

How do I lock the app?

Settings › Privacy › Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID). Manna locks whenever you leave the app.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual entries or a whole baby in the app. To remove everything, go to iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Manna, then delete the app.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 18 or later
  • An iCloud account for sync and family sharing (optional)
  • Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later (optional, Manna Family)

Manna is for tracking and general information only and is not medical advice. Always talk with your pediatrician or doctor about your baby’s and your own health.
