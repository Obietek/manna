Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Manna: Baby & Mom (“Manna” or “the app”) is made by Obietek. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no accounts, and we never see your data.

What the app stores

Your logs

Feeds, sleep, diapers, growth, health notes, medicines, milestones and photos you add for your baby, and the pregnancy, recovery, mood, water and journal entries you add for yourself, are stored on your device and synced to your private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit. Obietek cannot access this data. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your data stays on that device only.

Settings

Your units, name for “logged by,” reminders, theme and app lock choice are stored only on your device.

Family sharing (optional)

When you invite a partner or caregiver, Apple’s iCloud shares that baby’s records with the people you choose, using CloudKit sharing. Your own mom entries (mood, recovery, journal, pregnancy) are never shared. You can change roles or stop sharing at any time in Settings › Family sharing.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on in Settings › Privacy, Manna writes the mother’s weight and water intake to Apple Health. Manna does not read your Health data, and your baby’s data is never written to Health.

CategoryWhy
Body weight (write only)To save the weights you log in the Mom tab to Apple Health.
Water (write only)To save the cups of water you log in the Mom tab to Apple Health.

You can change this at any time in Health › Sharing › Apps › Manna.

Widgets, Live Activities and Apple Watch

A small summary (last feed, last diaper, sleep status) is kept in a private storage container on your iPhone that only Manna and its own widgets can read. Running timers are shown to your paired Apple Watch over Apple’s Watch Connectivity, a direct, encrypted device-to-device channel. The watch app also syncs through your own iCloud.

Notifications, Face ID and photos

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Face ID or Touch ID is used only to unlock the app; Manna never receives your biometric data. Photos you attach are stored with your logs in your iCloud as described above.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your data on any server of ours. The app has no account.
  • We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising or tracking, or include any third-party analytics or advertising tools.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never disclosed to third parties.

Purchases

Manna is free. The optional Manna Family upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple; we do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID.

Your data and Apple

Data in iCloud and Apple Health is managed by Apple according to your settings. See Apple’s privacy policy. To delete your data, delete each baby and entry in the app (shared babies are removed for everyone you shared with), or remove Manna’s data in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

Manna is used by parents and caregivers to record information about their own babies. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children who use it.

Health disclaimer

Manna is for tracking and general information only. Guides, wake windows and growth percentiles (based on WHO growth standards) are not medical advice, and Manna is not a medical device. Always talk with your pediatrician or doctor about your baby’s and your own health. If you are in crisis, call or text the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline at 1-833-852-6262, or call 911.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the Manna support page. Please don’t include personal or health information in a public issue.
