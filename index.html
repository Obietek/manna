Feeds, sleep and diapers in two taps.

Manna is a calm, simple tracker for babies and moms. Log the day in a couple of taps, see patterns at a glance, care for yourself, and share it all with your partner. Short King James verses meet you along the way, from the 3 a.m. feed to the pediatrician visit.

“It is the bread which the LORD hath given you to eat.” — Exodus 16:15

What it does

  • Breastfeeding timer with left and right sides, bottles, pumping and a milk stash
  • Diapers, sleep and naps, solids, temperature, medicine reminders and growth with WHO percentiles
  • “Time since” tiles, day, week and month insights, and wake windows by age
  • Mom tools: pregnancy week, kick counter, contraction timer, recovery, mood, water and a journal
  • Live Activities, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri, and an Apple Watch app
  • Twins and multiple children, Face ID app lock and night mode
Free. No account. No ads. Your data stays in your own iCloud. Manna Family is an optional one-time $9.99 upgrade for partner and caregiver sharing, the Apple Watch app, all widgets, long-range trends, a pediatrician PDF, and themes. No subscription, and caregivers you invite never pay.
